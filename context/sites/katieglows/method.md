## Why thirty days, honestly
Not a marketing number. Your skin replaces itself on a turnover cycle, and that cycle slows as we age. The face you see this morning was largely built weeks ago. Anything you start today cannot report back for weeks; most people quit one week in. This plan is built on the timescale your skin actually works on.
