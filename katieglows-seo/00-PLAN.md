# katieglows.com SEO article set

Twelve long-tail articles for women 50–70 searching for the exact things Katie's videos talk about
(wall sits, simple skincare, morning routines, looking good without procedures). Every article ends at
`/method`. Paste each article into the site as its own page at the slug shown.

## How to use these files

- One file per article: `01-…md` to `12-…md`. Each starts with a block of page settings
  (title tag, meta description, slug, H1) followed by the article body in Markdown.
- `ALL-ARTICLES.md` is the same content in one file if it's easier to paste in one go.
- Internal links point at the slugs below. If you change a slug, change the links that point to it.
- The CTA box at the end of every article describes The Timeless Method accurately ($9, 28-page PDF, four
  habits added one week at a time, 7-day refund, no products to buy) and links to `/method`. Edit the wording
  once and copy it to the others if you want it changed.
- Add one photo of Katie near the top of each page (any still from the BANK clips works) with the alt
  text suggested in the file. Google likes an image; the page also looks less like a wall of text.

## Site-level things worth doing (5 minutes each)

1. Put a "Articles" or "Tips" link in the site nav so the pages are crawlable from the homepage.
2. Each article page: one H1 only, the title tag and meta description from the file, and a canonical URL.
3. Link to the articles from the `/method` page ("Read more: …") so link equity flows both ways.
4. Add the articles to the sitemap, then submit the sitemap in Google Search Console (free).
5. Pin one article link in the Katie TikTok/IG bios in rotation; those are real visits Google notices.

## Voice

Warm, plain, editorial "we" voice from the Katie Glows brand. No invented personal medical history,
no "I lost 20 lb" style testimony, no promises of results. Facts are sourced; the sources are listed
at the bottom of each article so you can keep or remove them.
