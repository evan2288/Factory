# DM templates

Short, because clippers get a lot of DMs. Personalise the first line every time (their page, a clip of
theirs you saw). Never mass-DM inside a server that bans it.

---

## 1. Cold outreach to a clipper you've seen posting well

> Hey [name] — saw your [brand/campaign] clips, the [specific thing, e.g. "text-on-face hooks"] are clean.
> I run campaigns for skincare/wellness brands where the clips are already cut and captioned, you just post.
> [RATE]/1k verified views, tier-1 pages, paid twice a month. Want the brief? Takes 2 min to apply:
> [APPLY LINK]

## 2. Follow-up (3 days later, once only)

> Quick bump — the [brand] campaign is live now, first payouts go out [date]. If you'd rather see the
> clips before applying, here's a preview folder: [preview link]. No pressure either way.

## 3. Accepted

> You're in ✅ Welcome to memecpm.
> Server: [SERVER INVITE] → read #rules once, then grab a campaign in #campaigns.
> First step: post 3 clips from the [brand] folder this week and submit the links in the form. I'll check
> them at 72 h and tell you which angle to lean into. Payouts: twice a month via [PAYOUT METHOD].

## 4. Not accepted (audience or page issue)

> Thanks for applying. Right now your page is under the 40% tier-1 audience the brands require, so I can't
> add you to paid campaigns yet. If that changes (or you have another page that qualifies), send the
> analytics screenshot and I'll take another look. No hard feelings.

## 5. Clip rejected (specific reason)

> Heads up: `[clip link]` won't count for the [brand] campaign — [reason: under 10 s / no captions / brand
> tag missing / likes hidden / not from the campaign folder]. Fix and repost and it'll count from the new
> post date. Everything else you submitted is fine.

## 6. Payout notice

> Payout sent 💸 [period]: [X] verified views × [RATE] = $[amount] via [PAYOUT METHOD].
> Top clip: `[clip]` at [views]. Next payout [date]. Keep everything live — the 30-day check is on [date].

## 7. Warning (rule breach, first time)

> Noticed `[post]` was boosted / reposted across your other pages / deleted before 30 days. That's a
> campaign rule; this one's a warning and the post won't count. A second one means removal from paid
> campaigns. Rules are in #rules if you want to re-read them.

## 8. Brand-side: pitching a founder on the pay-per-view plan

> Hi [name] — I run memecpm. We turn footage a brand already has into ~200 clips a month and post them
> across our pages and a vetted clipper community, and you pay $2.50 per 1,000 verified views (vs $10–30
> on Meta). Our own pages have done $10k in tracked Amazon sales organically. Happy to cut 25 clips from
> one of your videos for $200 so you can see it: memecpm.com
