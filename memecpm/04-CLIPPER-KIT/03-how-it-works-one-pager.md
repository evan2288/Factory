# memecpm clipping — how it works (one page for clippers)

**What it is.** Brands pay memecpm to get their product seen. We cut their footage into clips, you post
the clips on your pages, and you're paid for every 1,000 verified views. You don't need to edit anything
(you can if you want to), you don't need a dedicated page, and you don't need to talk to the brand.

## The loop

1. **Apply once** at [APPLY LINK]: your page links and a screenshot of your audience analytics. Approved
   in 48 hours if your page has a majority tier-1 audience (US, UK, CA, AU) and looks real.
2. **Pick a campaign** in #campaigns. The brief tells you: the brand, the rate per 1,000 views, the
   exact caption line and tag, what's allowed, and the Drive folder with the clips.
3. **Post.** Use the clips as-is or put your own spin on them (your voiceover, your caption style, a
   reaction). Rules that always apply: longer than 10 seconds, captions on, the brand tag in the
   caption, no watermarks from other apps.
4. **Submit** the post link in the campaign's form within 30 minutes of posting.
5. **Leave it up 30 days.** Views are recorded at 72 hours and again at 30 days. Deleting early = the
   post doesn't count.
6. **Get paid** twice a month via [PAYOUT METHOD]. Your payout message shows every post, its verified
   views, and the maths.

## What "verified views" means

Platform-reported views on a post that: is from the campaign folder, follows the caption rules, is
longer than 10 seconds, has captions, is still live at 30 days, has likes visible, has engagement at or
above the floor on the brief (usually 0.2–1%), and wasn't boosted with paid promotion. Views from
stories, reposts across your own pages, or bot traffic don't count.

## Rates and bonuses

- Standard rate: [RATE] per 1,000 verified views. Some campaigns pay more; the brief always says.
- Top 3 clippers on each campaign by verified views get a bonus (amount on the brief).
- Consistent clippers with strong numbers get early access to new campaigns and higher-rate briefs.

## Instant rejection (so you never lose a post to a technicality)

- Clip 10 seconds or shorter
- No captions/subtitles
- Missing the brand tag or required caption line
- Footage not from the campaign folder
- Hidden likes
- Deleted before 30 days
- Boosted with TikTok/Instagram promote or any paid ads
- The same clip posted across several of your pages (post different clips on different pages)
- Story views (they don't count)

## Why memecpm campaigns are easier than most

- The clips are already cut, captioned and hooked. Your time per post is a couple of minutes.
- Every Monday we post which hooks are winning, so you know what to post next.
- Clips are made in variants (different opening hooks and text styles), so several clippers can post the
  same moment without it looking like a repost.
- The brands are real, the sales are tracked, and the campaigns keep running: the network has already
  produced $10k+ in tracked orders for brands.
