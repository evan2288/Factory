## What to do

1. Open the content folder. Clips are grouped by angle (`01-before-after/`, `02-demo/`, `03-quotes/` …),
   each with 2–3 hook variants. `TRACKER.xlsx` lists every clip with its hook text and a suggested
   caption.
2. Post clips to your page(s). Use them as they are, or add your own voice, reaction, or caption style.
3. Caption: include the required line and tag above. Keep it short and native; the suggested captions
   in the tracker are a starting point.
4. Submit each post link in the form within 30 minutes.
5. Keep posts live for 30 days. Views are logged at 72 hours and 30 days.

## ✅ Do

- Strong first second: the clips already open on the hook; don't add an intro before it
- Captions on every clip (they're burned in; if you re-edit, keep them)
- Post different clips on different pages
- Make it feel native, not like an ad
- Read the comments on the brand's own videos: the most-liked comments tell you which moments people
  loved; those angles are in the folder already

## ❌ Don't (instant rejection)

- Footage from anywhere except the content folder
- Clips 10 seconds or shorter
- Missing caption line or tag
- Claims not on the approved list below
- Watermarks from other apps
- Hidden likes · deleted before 30 days · boosted/promoted posts · story posting · reposting the same
  clip across your pages
