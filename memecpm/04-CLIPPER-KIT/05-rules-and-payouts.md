# memecpm clipper rules and payouts

*Pinned in #rules. These apply to every campaign; a campaign brief can add rules but never remove these.*

## 1. Pages

- Your page must show a **majority tier-1 audience (US, UK, CA, AU), 40% or more** in your platform
  analytics. We check at approval and spot-check during campaigns.
- Pages must be real: real followers, likes visible, no bought engagement. Suspected bot traffic removes
  the page from paid campaigns.
- Non-dedicated pages are fine unless a brief says otherwise. Networks and large meme pages only where a
  brief allows them.

## 2. Posts

- Only clips from the campaign's content folder. Nothing from other sources.
- Longer than 10 seconds. Captions on. Required caption line and brand tag in the caption.
- **#ad** or the platform's paid-partnership label on every post. This is a legal requirement (FTC/ASA),
  not a preference.
- English unless the brief says otherwise.
- Stay live 30 days. Early deletion = post doesn't count, and repeated early deletion = removal.
- No watermarks from other apps. No re-uploads of other clippers' posts.

## 3. Not allowed (removal from paid campaigns)

- Paid boosting or promotion of campaign posts
- Story posting to farm views (story views never count)
- The same clip across several of your own pages
- Bait posting: unrelated viral content with a campaign tag
- Hidden likes
- Claims not on the approved list, competitor attacks, NSFW, drama-bait
- Contacting the brand directly

## 4. Verification

- Submit each post within **30 minutes of posting** using the campaign form.
- Views are recorded from the platform's public count at **72 hours** and at **30 days**. The 30-day
  number is what's paid.
- Engagement must meet the floor on the brief. Posts under the floor at 30 days are not paid.
- We may request an analytics screenshot for any post. No screenshot within 48 hours = post not paid.
- Disputes: DM evannn4323 within 7 days of the payout message with the post link. We'll look at the
  platform data together.

## 5. Payouts

- Rate per 1,000 verified views is on each brief. Standard: [RATE].
- Paid **twice a month** ([1st and 16th]) via [PAYOUT METHOD] for all posts that passed their 30-day
  check in that period.
- Minimum payout: $[10]. Balances under that roll to the next period.
- Bonuses for the top 3 clippers per campaign are paid with the campaign's final payout.
- You're responsible for your own taxes.

## 6. Strikes

- First breach: warning, post doesn't count.
- Second breach: removed from paid campaigns for 30 days.
- Fraud (bots, fake analytics, boosting): permanent removal, unpaid balance forfeited.

## 7. Content and rights

- Clips are provided for the campaign only. Don't reuse them for other brands or after the campaign
  closes, unless the brief says the clips are evergreen.
- By posting you confirm you have the right to post on that page and that you'll follow the platform's
  rules on paid partnerships.
