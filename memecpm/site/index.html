How it works

From one video to a bank of clips in 7 days

Send footage

A founder video, product demos, UGC you own, a podcast, a YouTube channel, a livestream. Drop it in a shared folder with the claims you're allowed to make.

I find the moments

Every source is transcribed and scored for hooks: the quote, the demo, the before-and-after, the number, the objection answered. The top moments get cut.

Each cut becomes three clips

Reframed to 9:16 with the speaker tracked in frame, word-timed captions, an on-screen hook in the first second, a music bed, and three different opening hooks so we can test what holds attention.

You get a bank, not a zip file

Clips land in a Drive folder organised by source and week, with a tracker sheet: hook text, suggested caption, status dropdown, who posted it where. Your team, or your AI tools, post straight from it.

Or I post them

On the Distribution plans the clips go out daily across my page network and a vetted clipper community, with a free guide and comment-to-DM automation that carries your link. You see views, clicks and the top performers every week.

FAQ
What counts as a clip?

A finished, posting-ready 9:16 video, 8–60 seconds, cut from your footage at a moment chosen for its hook, with the speaker kept in frame, captions, an on-screen hook line and a music bed where it fits. Each cut ships with two or three different opening hooks; every variant counts as one clip, because each is a separate post.

Who owns the clips?

You do, outright, once the invoice is paid. I keep the right to show them as examples unless you say no.

Where do the clips get posted on the Distribution plans?

On pages I run and on vetted clippers' pages, not on your accounts (unless you ask). Every paid post carries #ad or a paid-partnership label.

What is a "verified view"?

A platform-reported view on a post that stayed live for 30 days, from a page with a majority tier-1 audience, with engagement above the platform floor and no paid boosting. Same rules the large clipping campaigns use. I log them at 72 hours and 30 days and you see the log.

Can I use the clips in paid ads?

Yes, on every plan.

What if a clip says something we can't claim?

Your brief lists approved and banned claims. Every batch is checked against it before delivery, and anything that slips through is replaced.

How fast?

First batch within 7 days of the brief. Weekly batches after that. Rush to 48 hours is an add-on.

What do you need from me?

The footage (or links), the product link, the claims you're allowed to make, and one round of notes on the first batch. After that, most clients spend under an hour a month on it.
